# Planning

The way this subdirectory is structured is numerically ascending, in terms of 
planning revisions/iterations. Folder `0/` is the first attempt at planning the 
document, including the general structure, as well as the first mental draft. 
The subsequent directories are more complete and should reflect the thought 
process of iterative planning stages.

They may be accompanied with supplementary planning documents to give a better 
view of I was thinking as I was drafting the plan.

NOTE: In practice, I probably would not save every stage of the planning, I 
would probably delete or overwrite initial planning with their revised 
counterparts. But I am collecting them in sequence like this so you can see 
what I was thinking as a reader.
